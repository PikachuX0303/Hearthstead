# Third-party content

## MineColonies building blueprints

The hut blueprints in `src/main/resources/data/hearthstead/blueprints/` (and in the released jar
at `data/hearthstead/blueprints/`) come from the style packs of
[MineColonies](https://github.com/ldtteam/minecolonies), by the LDT Team and the style pack
builders named below. MineColonies is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3
(GPL-3.0-only); the same license text is in `LICENSE`.

They were taken from a MineColonies jar and converted with `tools/import_blueprints.py`:
MineColonies, Structurize and Domum Ornamentum blocks were replaced by vanilla Minecraft blocks
and Hearthstead hut blocks, and only the hut blueprints were kept. Because these files are
GPL-3.0-only, Hearthstead as a whole is distributed under GPL-3.0-only.

Hearthstead is not affiliated with or endorsed by the LDT Team.
